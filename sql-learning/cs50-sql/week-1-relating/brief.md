## Most Interesting Assignment: Moneyball

The **Moneyball** assignment explored how relational databases can be used to answer real-world questions by analyzing historical baseball data across multiple related tables.

The assignment focused on retrieving meaningful insights by combining information from different datasets, reinforcing the importance of relationships, joins, subqueries, grouping, and filtering when working with relational databases.

### Specification 12: `12.sql`

This query identifies the player who earned the lowest cost per hit during the 2001 season.

To produce the result, it combines salary data with player performance statistics, performs the necessary calculations, and ranks the results to determine the most cost-effective player based on salary per hit.

It demonstrates the use of:
- INNER JOIN
- Filtering records
- Arithmetic calculations
- Aliasing columns
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT
